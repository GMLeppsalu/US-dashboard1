Interpretation Guide for Equity Research ▼
  • Withheld income tax trends — Proxy for aggregate wage & employment growth. Rising withheld taxes suggest strengthening labor market and wage inflation.
  • Non-withheld individual taxes — Proxy for capital gains realizations and self-employment income. Surges often correlate with equity market gains and tax-planning events.
  • Corporate income taxes — Proxy for corporate profitability. Compare trends to S&P 500 earnings revisions for leading/confirming signals.
  • Excise taxes — Consumption and activity indicator. Tracks gasoline usage, air travel, tobacco/alcohol, and other specific economic activities.
  • Customs duties — Import volume and trade activity indicator. Sensitive to tariff policy changes and global trade flows.
  • Social insurance receipts — Employment breadth indicator. Tracks the total payroll base (number of workers × wages subject to FICA).
Data Sources: US Treasury Fiscal Data API (api.fiscaldata.treasury.gov). Monthly Treasury Statement (MTS) Table 4 — updated monthly, ~8-12th business day of the following month. Daily Treasury Statement (DTS) Table II — updated daily by 4 PM next business day.
Units: All dollar amounts are in millions of USD unless otherwise noted. Chart values in $B are converted from source data.
YoY Calculation: ((Current − Prior) / |Prior|) × 100. Division-by-zero cases are shown as N/A.
Note: DTS Table II was restructured on Feb 14, 2023. Category names for pre-Feb 2023 data may differ. Social Insurance receipts combine on-budget and off-budget components.
